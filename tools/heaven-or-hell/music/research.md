# Style research: "Lil Tony Official"-inspired beat for Heaven Or Hell

Researched 2026-10-23 for Dan's brief: a heavenly beat in the style of Lil Tony Official that switches up into an evil version as corruption rises.

This is **inspired-by only**. No melody, sample, vocal, tag, drum loop or motif from any real record is used. Every note in the score is original, and every sound is synthesised from oscillators and noise in `tools/heaven-or-hell/music/synth/`. The artist's name and voice appear nowhere in the audio.

## Who is Lil Tony Official?

The name is not ambiguous. **Lil Tony** (also billed as *Lil Tony Official*, *Key* or *TKey*) is Tekai Elijah Key, born around 2004. He is a rapper **and record producer** from Atlanta's Westside, signed to Priority Records [1][4].

- He started recording on his phone during the COVID lockdowns. The 2023 singles "3AM" and "Canoozled" and the mixtape *2 Sides 2 Every Story* (November 2023) broke him nationally [1][4].
- He is "a longtime self-producer" who co-produced over half of the *Born Again (Deluxe)* tracks [3]. Credits on *Born Again* list "Key" as a producer alongside outside producers [8].
- In 2025 he turned toward faith. *Born Again* is a Christian-leaning trap album that Pitchfork ranked among the best rap albums of 2025 and described as "the bible-thumping motivation trap of Born Again," more about "the trauma and struggles that led him down a faith-based path" [5]. Its intro line, "Went through hell and fought with all my demons," is quoted there [5].

Both halves of Dan's brief map onto real phases of his catalogue. The "heaven" side is *Born Again* era: faith-themed, "chill and vibey" beats [7]. The "hell" side is the 2023 era: "mean mumbles and eerie ATL underground beats" [2] and the "minimal moodiness of a horror flick" [4]. A 2023 tape is even titled *2 Sides 2 Every Story* [4], so a two-faced, same-song switch-up suits him.

| 808 | Tune the 808 to the key so slides land on chord tones. Sidechain-duck the 808 under the kick, and let the 808 own everything below 80 Hz. Soft-clip the 808 for bite. | [11] |

### Honest limits

- I could not listen to audio. Everything here comes from written descriptions, credits and tempo and key tags on type-beat listings, not from transcribing his records. That is also why nothing can have been copied.
- Lil Tony works with many producers: Comet, Four3va, whyceg, Hariroc, Deeski and others [8][14]. There is no single "Lil Tony producer sound." The *type-beat* convention sketched by the guides [11] is a community abstraction, and one guide is a plugin vendor's blog.
- No interview I found has him describing his own drum or 808 programming. BPM and key claims come from third-party type-beat listings, not his masters.
- The closest well-documented styles I actually based this on are **Atlanta underground trap and dark plugg** for the evil set [4][6][9][10], and **melodic, ambient, faith-themed trap** for the heavenly set [2][3][5][7]. The tempo, drum and 808 conventions come from the type-beat guide [11] and the general trap description [13].

## How the research shaped the score

- **150 BPM with a half-time feel (75)**, straight grid with light 16th swing on the hats only [11].
- **Key centre F** for an 808 that translates [11]. Heaven is **F Lydian** (bright, with the raised 4th B♮). Hell is **F Phrygian with a Phrygian-dominant C** [11]. It is the same tonic, so both sets share a bass pedal and the drums never change grid.
- **The heaven set** is "quiet, nearly ambient" [2], built from choir pads, FM bells and harp plucks, a soft 808 and sparse hats. It nods to the *Born Again* "heavenly rap" brief.
- **The corruption layers** follow "simmering in moodiness before exploding into chaos" [4]. Density and distortion rise as layers are added: triplet hat rolls with velocity ramps [11], a detuned and bitcrushed choir, a tritone counter-line, reversed swells, and a gritty 808 with slides.
- **The hell set** follows the "shiny horns and sinkhole-inducing bass" and "horror flick" moodiness [4][9]. It uses menacing low bells, low brass and organ stabs, a distorted sliding 808, aggressive rolls, Phrygian ♭II and a sinister choir.
- **The switch-up** uses a riser plus a low-pass filter sweep into a drum drop on a phrase boundary [11].
