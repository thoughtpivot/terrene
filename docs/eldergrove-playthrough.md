# Eldergrove Quest Playthrough Guide

## 🎮 How to Play

### Starting the Game

1. **Access the game** at: `https://bc-4a65ff4e-a087-4d83-91e6-30893173a4a6--9000.portal.cursor.com`
2. From the main menu, click **"Eldergrove"**

### Controls

- **Arrow Keys / WASD**: Move player
- **E**: Interact with NPCs and investigate objects
- **Y**: Accept quests
- **Space**: Advance dialogue / Complete quest turn-ins
- **Click**: Alternative interaction method

## 📖 Complete Quest Walkthrough

### Quest 1: The Lost Heirloom

#### Step 1: Meet Elder Rowan
- Navigate to the **center of the village**
- Look for **Elder Rowan** (center position, ~768, 400)
- You'll see a **yellow "!"** above his head
- Press **E** to talk
- Press **Space** to advance through dialogue
- Press **Y** to accept the quest

#### Step 2: Talk to Guard Captain
- Find the **Guard Captain** near the village entrance (west side, ~600, 500)
- The yellow indicator shows he's your next objective
- Press **E** to talk
- He'll tell you about the ruins and mark them on your "map"

#### Step 3: Investigate the Ruins
- Travel **east** to the ruins marker (~1300, 400)
- You'll see a translucent marker/area
- When you get close, the prompt "[E] Investigate Ruins" appears
- Press **E** to investigate
- Read the findings about ancient markings

#### Step 4: Return to Elder Rowan
- Go back to **Elder Rowan** in the village center
- You'll see a **green "?"** above his head (quest complete indicator)
- Press **E** to talk
- Press **Space** to advance through completion dialogue
- Quest complete! You receive rewards.

---

### Quest 2: Shadows Gathering

#### Step 1: Talk to Elder Rowan Again
- After completing Quest 1, Elder Rowan now has **another yellow "!"**
- This is the second quest in the chain
- Press **E** to talk
- Press **Y** to accept "Shadows Gathering"

#### Step 2: Find the Mysterious Stranger
- Look for the **Mysterious Stranger** near the old well (east side, ~950, 600)
- They'll have a yellow indicator
- Press **E** to talk
- They'll give you locations of 3 clues to find

#### Step 3: Collect the 3 Clues

**Clue 1: Charred Journal** 
- Location: Near the **old oak tree** (west side, ~400, 300)
- Look for a brown translucent marker
- Press **E** when close: "Search Old Oak"
- Clue collected (1/3)

**Clue 2: Claw Marks**
- Location: At the **ancient well** (east side, ~1000, 600)
- Look for a gray translucent marker
- Press **E** when close: "Examine Well"
- Clue collected (2/3)

**Clue 3: Ash** (from ruins entrance - auto-collected if you haven't triggered it separately)
- If needed, check near the ruins again

#### Step 4: Return to Mysterious Stranger
- Once you have all 3 clues, return to the **Mysterious Stranger**
- They'll now have a **green "?"** indicator
- Press **E** to hear what the clues mean

#### Step 5: Final Report to Elder Rowan
- Return to **Elder Rowan** one last time
- He'll have the green "?" indicator
- Press **E** to complete the quest chain
- Receive your final rewards!

| Gray **...** | Active quest - still working on objectives |

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Quest not appearing
- Make sure you accepted the first quest by pressing **Y**
- Check that yellow "!" indicators are showing
- Try talking to Elder Rowan again

### Can't find quest markers
- Quest markers are translucent colored rectangles on the ground
- Get close (within ~40-50 pixels) to see the interaction prompt
- Look for the "[E]" prompt when nearby

### Clues not collecting
- Make sure Quest 2 is **active** (not just available)
- Check you haven't already collected that specific clue
- Stand directly on the marker and press **E**

### Progress not saving
- Quest progress auto-saves to browser localStorage
- Refreshing the page should maintain your progress
- Clearing browser data will reset quests
